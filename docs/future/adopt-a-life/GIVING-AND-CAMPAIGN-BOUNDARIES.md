# Adopt a Life — Giving and Campaign Boundaries

> FUTURE ONLY. This file records the decided relationship between Adopt a Life and campaigns.

## 1. Two giving modes

Interplanetary Fund's intended model has two principal forms of giving:

### Campaign giving

**Donation for a purpose.**

The campaign identifies the goal/purpose for which support is being requested.

### Adopt a Life giving

**Purposeless giving to support a life.**

The giver supports a person or eligible organization-managed animal profile without requiring a stated spending purpose.

## 2. No giver-imposed restrictions

Givers may not attach enforceable or platform-managed restrictions, stipulations, spending instructions, conditions, or control rights to either a campaign donation or an Adopt a Life gift.

A voluntary note, encouragement, or thank-you interaction must not be converted into a restriction on the recipient's use of funds.

If a future product needs restricted purchasing, item fulfillment, escrow, or designated-goods purchasing, that must be implemented as a separate product type rather than changing these giving rules.

## 3. Organizations must campaign for organization-level giving

Organizations that want donations for themselves, their operations, programs, or a stated purpose must create a campaign.

Adopt a Life is for support directed to a specific person or eligible organization-managed animal profile, not unrestricted organization fundraising.

## 4. Animal boundary

Approved animal organizations may maintain animal Adopt a Life profiles.

An ordinary individual who wants financial help for a particular personal animal/pet must create a campaign instead of creating an animal Adopt a Life profile.

## 5. Campaign capacity rule

Unless an active subscription grants additional capacity:

- 1 campaign may be live at a time;
- campaigns may be paused and later unpaused;
- up to 2 campaign drafts may exist in addition to the 1 live campaign;
- maximum without subscription = 3 campaigns total: 2 drafts + 1 live.

The subscription system may raise these limits according to separately documented subscription rules.

An Adopt a Life profile is not a campaign and should not consume a campaign slot unless a future explicit decision changes this.

## 6. Out-of-scope future sibling concept: Buy a Neighbor a Basket

A separate future branch/product may eventually support a third type of giving involving actual goods rather than unrestricted money.

Recorded concept:

- recipient chooses/assigns a participating store where they can pick up items;
- giver pays through Interplanetary Fund;
- Interplanetary Fund facilitates payment/order fulfillment with the store;
- recipient receives a platform message when the order is paid and ready for pickup;
- this is intended for examples such as buying a neighbor a basket of needed items.

This idea is **not part of Adopt a Life** and must not be implemented through donor restrictions on Adopt a Life gifts or campaigns. If pursued, it should become a separate sibling under the neutral `future` branch with its own specifications, payments/legal review, fulfillment design, and fraud controls.

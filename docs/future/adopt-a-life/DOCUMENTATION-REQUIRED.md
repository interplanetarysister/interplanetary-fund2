# Adopt a Life — Documentation Still Required

> FUTURE ONLY. This is the consolidated documentation backlog for Adopt a Life. Keep this list together so implementation does not begin without the required product, legal, safety, and operational records.

All legal/contract documents must prioritize lawful protection of **Interplanetary Fund, its owners, developers, administrators, employees/contractors, affiliates, and applicable service providers**, while avoiding unenforceable language or claims that a waiver removes duties that cannot legally be waived.

## A. Human profile consent and control documents

### 1. Human Profile Creation & Control Authorization
Required when one account holder creates/controls an Adopt a Life profile for another person.

Must address:

- subject's consent to profile creation;
- subject's consent to account owner controlling the profile;
- authorization to publish submitted details/photos;
- right to withdraw/revoke consent;
- what happens to active donations/updates when consent is revoked;
- representations about submitted content;
- responsibility/liability allocation;
- electronic-signature validity/recording;
- acknowledgement that Interplanetary Fund hosts/facilitates the submitted information and does not independently verify every factual statement.

This must be signed **before profile details/photos can be added** for a proxy-created human profile.

### 2. Payout Method Authorization
Required for a proxy-created human profile before withdrawals are enabled.

Must identify/authorize the usable payout method approved by the profile subject and state that changing the payout destination may require renewed authorization.

### 3. Consent Revocation / Profile Control Change Form
Needed for a profile subject who withdraws consent, changes controllers, separates from an organization, or takes over control where permitted.

## B. Organization/business documents

### 4. Multi-Profile Organization Application
For businesses/nonprofits requesting permission to create/manage more than one Adopt a Life profile.

Must include:

- legal/entity details;
- EIN where applicable;
- organization type;
- requested human/animal/volunteer profile categories;
- rough estimate of number of active sponsorship/adoption profiles at any given time;
- account/controller details required by law/provider/platform rules;
- intended use;
- responsibility representations;
- consent-compliance representations;
- admin review fields;
- approval/denial record;
- continuing-documentation obligations.

### 5. Organization Profile Responsibility / Indemnity Agreement
For approved organizations creating profiles on behalf of others.

Must address responsibility for authorization, submitted profile content, consent collection, payout instructions, misuse, false statements, and compliance obligations.

### 6. Animal Organization Authority Agreement
For shelters/rescues managing animal Adopt a Life profiles.

Must confirm organizational authority over the animal/caretaking relationship and make clear that financial support is not legal adoption/custody transfer.

### 7. Homeless/Housing Organization Participant Safeguarding Addendum
For shelters/outreach/housing organizations managing human profiles.

Must prohibit conditioning shelter/services on Adopt a Life participation and address privacy, sensitive location information, consent, separation from the organization, and continued support after housing status changes.

## C. General account/profile terms

### 8. Adopt a Life Account Owner Waiver / Representations / Indemnity
Needed for self-created and proxy-created profiles as appropriate.

Must allocate responsibility for user-submitted profile/campaign content and false/unauthorized submissions while preserving all non-waivable obligations of Interplanetary Fund.

### 9. Adopt a Life Recipient/Controller Terms
Defines controller powers, withdrawal timing, profile management, donation acceptance, payout restrictions, suspension effects, and prohibited conduct.

### 10. Public Profile Content Consent
Covers photos/media, optional personal details, screen names, public updates, and consent to public display.

## D. Donor/giver documents

### 11. Adopt a Life Giver Disclosure & Terms
Must clearly state:

- gift supports the person/eligible organization-managed animal profile;
- no spending purpose is required;
- giver may not impose restrictions/stipulations;
- giver does not obtain ownership/control/guardianship/employment rights;
- screen name/public profile may not equal independently verified legal identity;
- Interplanetary Fund does not guarantee truth of user-submitted profile content unless expressly verified;
- fees/refunds/payment rules;
- anonymity choices;
- suspension/fund-hold implications where lawfully applicable.

### 12. Campaign Giver Restriction Disclosure Update
Existing campaign donor terms should explicitly confirm that donors may not attach platform-enforced spending restrictions to gifts, even though the campaign itself states a purpose.

### 13. Anonymous Giving Disclosure
Defines what “anonymous” means publicly versus what Interplanetary Fund/processors may still know or disclose when legally required.

## E. Payout/non-bank documents

### 14. Reloadable/Prepaid Card Compatibility Disclosure
Must state:

- Interplanetary Fund does not issue/provide cards;
- recipient obtains their own card;
- listed/recommended compatible providers are informational, not guaranteed endorsements;
- compatibility can change;
- card/payout provider terms apply;
- unsupported cards cannot be used as payout destinations.

### 15. Payout Eligibility & Withdrawal Requirements
One authoritative document describing when donations may be received versus when withdrawals are enabled.

### 16. Electronic Signature Standard
Vendor-neutral standard for signed consent/payout authorizations: signer attribution, timestamp, tamper-evidence, retention, auditability, revocation, and accepted providers. DocuSign is a candidate but not yet selected.

## F. Moderation/enforcement documents

### 17. Report User Form
Must capture reason, evidence, screenshots/attachments as allowed, acknowledgement against malicious reporting, and reporter identity/account linkage.

### 18. Admin Suspension Review Worksheet
For mandatory admin reasoning, evidence review, suspension count, disposition, and audit record.

### 19. Just-Cause Evaluation Form
For users who reach the final suspension stage to explain what happened and why the account should be restored/retained.

### 20. Malicious/Excessive Reporting Policy
Defines when reporting behavior itself becomes suspendable and how invalid reports are removed from target suspension history.

### 21. Permanent Ban / Account Termination Notice
Must state reason, applicable appeal/review status, payout/account-claim steps, deadlines, and lawful fee treatment if any.

### 22. Account Ownership Proof Standard
Defines what evidence is acceptable during the post-termination ownership-claim period.

### 23. Suspension-Fee Schedule and Disclosure
Only if legal review approves charging additional moderation/resource fees after permanent ban. Must define exact amounts/formula; no fees may be invented by code.

## G. Held funds, abandonment, and donor refund documents — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

### 24. Held Funds / Unclaimed Property Procedure
Jurisdiction-aware process for funds remaining after account loss/termination. Must address escheat/unclaimed-property obligations and must not assume Interplanetary Fund can take ownership.

### 25. 10-Day Final Response Notice
Notice for final just-cause response deadline.

### 26. 60-Day Account Ownership Claim Notice
Notice describing the additional ownership-proof period after account access forfeiture.

### 27. Donor Notice of Unresolved Recipient Funds
For donors with contact information if legally required/allowed after the recipient fails to complete the ownership/payout process.

### 28. Donor Refund/Return Request Form
For eligible donors to request return of affected funds during the legally approved claim window.

### 29. Final Disposition of Unclaimed Funds Policy
Must be drafted only after counsel determines what Interplanetary Fund may lawfully do with unresolved funds. The desired concept of eventual transfer to an Interplanetary Fund-owned account is **not approved for implementation until legally validated**.

## H. Privacy/safety documents

### 30. Adopt a Life Privacy & Public-Identity Policy
Screen-name-first display, non-public legal/payment data, organization-managed profile disclosures, and approximate-location rules.

### 31. Vulnerable Person / Minor Safeguarding Policy
Still required before minors or other specially protected categories are enabled.

### 32. Homelessness/Domestic-Violence Sensitive Location Standard
Defines prohibited/default-hidden location and case-management details.

### 33. Photo/Media Safety & Removal Procedure
Consent, removal, reporting, and organization-created media rules.

## I. Product/technical documentation still required

### 34. Final Product Specification
Complete screens, flows, role permissions, field-level rules, search/filter behavior, states, errors, and accessibility requirements.

### 35. Standardized Adopt a Life Profile Field Specification
Exact fields for human, volunteer, organization-managed human, and animal profiles.

### 36. UI/UX Layout Specification
Standard Adopt Me frame, Adopt Now button, human/animal visual distinction, organization relationship, donation flow, screen-name display, and mobile/web behavior.

### 37. Data Model & State Machine
Entities, IDs, consent records, profile-controller relationships, payout state, organization relationships, donation state, suspension state, audit records, and retention rules.

### 38. Permissions / Authorization Matrix
Exactly who may create, edit, publish, pause, withdraw, change payout methods, manage organization profiles, moderate, and view sensitive records.

### 39. Payment & Ledger Specification
Separate organization campaign money from individual Adopt a Life support; no donor restrictions; pending payout balances; reloadable-card support; refunds; fees; chargebacks; audit ledger.

### 40. Search / Discovery / Volunteer Filter Specification
Search behavior, volunteer option, local discovery, privacy-safe location, ranking/fairness rules, and organization relationships.

### 41. Inbox / Following / Thank-You Messaging Specification
No general DM system; followed updates to inbox; heart interactions; limited thank-you exception; non-user donor account prompt.

### 42. Moderation State Machine Specification
Automatic report suspension, suspension ladder, malicious-report handling, admin actions, fund holds, final evaluation, restoration, and ban states.

### 43. Lifecycle / Edge Cases
Profile transfer, organization separation, housed status change, animal adoption/transfer/death, user death/incapacity, consent withdrawal, duplicate profiles, account loss, recurring support, and payout-destination changes.

### 44. Implementation Boundary / Feature Flag Plan
How Adopt a Life stays isolated from production and from sibling future features until explicitly approved.

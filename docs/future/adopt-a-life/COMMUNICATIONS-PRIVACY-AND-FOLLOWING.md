# Adopt a Life — Communications, Privacy, and Following

> FUTURE ONLY. These rules record intended user interactions for later implementation.

## 1. No general user-to-user private messaging

Interplanetary Fund should not provide general private/direct messaging between users.

Normal communication should occur through:

- the Community forum;
- campaign/profile updates;
- heart/reaction interactions on updates;
- platform inbox/notification delivery of followed updates;
- limited donation thank-you communication described below.

## 2. Following

Following a campaign or applicable Adopt a Life profile subscribes the follower to voluntary updates within Interplanetary Fund.

- updates are delivered to the user's Interplanetary Fund inbox/notification experience;
- following does not create a private chat channel;
- updates remain voluntary for the campaign/profile owner;
- followers may heart/react to appropriate updates.

## 3. Thank-you messages are the limited messaging exception

After a donation, the profile/campaign controller may send a short customizable thank-you message to the donor.

### Donor already has an Interplanetary Fund account

A personalized thank-you may be delivered directly to that donor's Interplanetary Fund inbox.

This is a limited donation-related message, not an open-ended direct-message conversation.

### Donor does not yet have an Interplanetary Fund account

After donation completion, the donor may be prompted to create an account as a giver/supporter.

If they create an account, they can receive the personalized thank-you message from the campaign/profile controller in their Interplanetary Fund inbox.

The donation itself must not be conditioned on creating an account unless a separate future legal/product decision explicitly requires that.

## 4. Anonymous support

Givers may support anonymously.

Public/default donor identity should use the giver's screen name rather than legal name.

Recipients/other users see only the screen name unless:

- the giver voluntarily exposed additional identity information in the public portion of their own profile/campaign; or
- disclosure is required by applicable law/provider rules to the appropriate party.

The product must distinguish public anonymity from internal/payment records. Payment processors/platform compliance may still require non-public legal/payment information.

## 5. Friend privacy

A user's friend relationships should be anonymous/hidden from other users by default. Other users should not be able to browse a person's friend list merely because they can view the person's public profile.

This rule should remain compatible with the broader Interplanetary Fund friend feature without exposing social-graph data unnecessarily.

## 6. Screen-name-first experience

For Adopt a Life, donors should primarily experience the recipient/profile through the screen name and public profile the subject/controller chose to present.

- legal identity is not automatically public;
- the transaction must still be tied internally to a stable profile/account ID;
- public screen-name use must not falsely imply Interplanetary Fund independently verified the person's real-world identity or story.

## 7. Community-first interaction

The platform should direct broader discussion, encouragement, and community engagement to the Community forum and public/profile update surfaces rather than private messaging.

The design goal is to preserve social connection without creating an unrestricted private communication channel between donors and recipients.

Interplanetary Fund accepts contributions from developers, designers, researchers, security reviewers, documentation authors, organizations, and contributors using authorized artificial-intelligence and automated development systems.

This Agreement establishes the rights necessary for Interplanetary Association to receive, evaluate, modify, maintain, protect, distribute, license, sublicense, commercialize, and otherwise develop Contributions as part of Interplanetary Fund while maintaining a documented chain of authorization for contributed material.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Contribution
“Contribution” means any work intentionally submitted for inclusion in, modification of, or use by the Project, including source code, object code, modifications, patches, documentation, designs, specifications, database structures, schemas, configurations, tests, workflows, security improvements, graphics, text, APIs, integrations, and other copyrightable or protectable material.

A Contribution may be submitted through GitHub, Base44, an authorized development environment, an approved automated system or AI agent, email, or another contribution channel designated by Interplanetary Association.

Material clearly identified in writing as being submitted solely for discussion, evaluation, or commentary and not for incorporation into the Project is not a Contribution.

### 2.2 Contributor
“Contributor” means the individual or legal entity that owns or is authorized to license a Contribution and intentionally submits or authorizes its submission to Interplanetary Fund.

### 2.3 Submit
“Submit” means an intentional electronic, written, or otherwise recorded communication delivered to Interplanetary Association or an authorized Interplanetary Fund contribution system for the purpose of contributing to the Project.
